## 2. [Squared Codegrees of Expanded Cliques](<02-expanded-cliques/README.md>)

[Read the PDF](<02-expanded-cliques/Squared Codegrees of Expanded Cliques.pdf>) · 12 pages

We determine the squared-codegree Turán density of the four-uniform expanded triangle as $9/32$, with stability towards an odd bipartite construction of part ratio $1:3$. We also obtain asymptotic estimates for expanded cliques in higher even uniformities.

## 3. [Induced Almost-Cliques and Edge Extremality in Triple Systems](<03-induced-almost-cliques/README.md>)

[Read the PDF](<03-induced-almost-cliques/Induced Almost-Cliques and Edge Extremality in Triple Systems.pdf>) · 12 pages

We determine the exact induced five-vertex almost-clique extremum for sufficiently large triple systems and its unique extremal construction. This gives counterexamples to simultaneous edge and induced-count extremality, together with improved induced almost-clique densities for every even order at least six.

[Verification materials](<03-induced-almost-cliques/verification.zip>)

## 4. [Uniform Turán densities of small stars](<04-uniform-stars/README.md>)

[Read the PDF](<04-uniform-stars/Uniform Turán densities of small stars.pdf>) · 12 pages

We determine the uniform Turán densities of the three-uniform stars with six, seven and eight leaves as $13/25$, $7/12$ and $31/49$. Palette optimization and exact certificates also give improved upper bounds for the stars with four and five leaves.

[Verification materials](<04-uniform-stars/verification.zip>)

## 5. [Extremal Degree Moments in Directed Graphs](<05-directed-degree-moments/README.md>)

[Read the PDF](<05-directed-degree-moments/Extremal Degree Moments in Directed Graphs.pdf>) · 13 pages

We determine squared out-degree extrema for sufficiently large digraphs avoiding any five-vertex tournament and prove a quantitative stability theorem. We also determine every nondecreasing convex out-degree extremum for an infinite family formed by bidirected joins of a directed triangle and directed edges.

[Verification materials](<05-directed-degree-moments/verification.zip>)

## 8. [Positive Codegree Bounds for Daisies and Graph Expansions](<08-positive-codegrees/README.md>)

[Read the PDF](<08-positive-codegrees/Positive Codegree Bounds for Daisies and Graph Expansions.pdf>) · 10 pages

We improve the positive codegree upper bound for the four-uniform daisy to $6/11$, narrowing the known interval to $[8/15,6/11]$. We also determine positive codegree densities of graph expansions from their chromatic numbers and obtain stability and finite clique-expansion results.

[Verification materials](<08-positive-codegrees/verification.zip>)

## 9. [Squared Codegrees of Even Four-Uniform Hypergraphs](<09-even-four-graphs/README.md>)

[Read the PDF](<09-even-four-graphs/Squared Codegrees of Even Four-Uniform Hypergraphs.pdf>) · 9 pages

We determine the asymptotic squared triple-codegree density within the class of even four-uniform hypergraphs as $31/64$. The paper also describes local distributions of near-extremal members and quantifies the parity defects required to exceed this value; the unrestricted forbidden-clique problem remains open.

[Verification materials](<09-even-four-graphs/verification.zip>)
