# Uniform Turán densities of small stars

**Jialin He** · Manuscript 4 · 12 pages

We determine the uniform Turán densities of the three-uniform stars with six, seven and eight leaves as $13/25$, $7/12$ and $31/49$. Palette optimization and exact certificates also give improved upper bounds for the stars with four and five leaves.
