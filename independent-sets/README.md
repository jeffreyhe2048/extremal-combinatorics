## 1. [Counterexamples for independent sets in linear regular hypergraphs](<01-linear-regular-counterexamples/README.md>)

[Read the PDF](<01-linear-regular-counterexamples/Counterexamples for independent sets in linear regular hypergraphs.pdf>) · 11 pages

We construct counterexamples to conjectured strong-independent-set bounds in linear regular uniform hypergraphs. A transversal-design lifting method also gives simultaneous strong and ordinary independent-set counterexamples in specified three-uniform degree ranges.

## 6. [Independent Sets in Regular Hypergraphs at Both Extremes](<06-regular-independent-sets/README.md>)

[Read the PDF](<06-regular-independent-sets/Independent Sets in Regular Hypergraphs at Both Extremes.pdf>) · 17 pages

We prove conjectured independent-set partition-function and coefficient bounds in intervals near both ends of the parameter range for regular uniform hypergraphs, with stability results. For degree two, the partition-function bound holds at every nonnegative activity.

## 7. [Independent Sets in 3-Uniform 3-Regular Hypergraphs](<07-three-uniform-three-regular/README.md>)

[Read the PDF](<07-three-uniform-three-regular/Independent Sets in 3-Uniform 3-Regular Hypergraphs.pdf>) · 16 pages

We prove that every simple three-uniform three-regular hypergraph on $n$ vertices has at most $253^{n/9}$ independent sets, with equality exactly for disjoint unions of the conjectured nine-vertex construction. The paper also proves a partition-function comparison and component stability throughout the activity interval $[2/3,5/3]$.

## 10. [Weighted Independent Sets in Latin Square Hypergraphs](<10-latin-weighted-counting/README.md>)

[Read the PDF](<10-latin-weighted-counting/Weighted Independent Sets in Latin Square Hypergraphs.pdf>) · 10 pages

We obtain a uniform two-term weighted independent-set expansion for Latin square hypergraphs and determine its sharp activity interval. We also identify the optimal exponential rate of the remaining contribution and the growth rate attainable by Latin lifts of a fixed base hypergraph.
