# Independent Sets in Regular Hypergraphs at Both Extremes

**Jialin He** · Manuscript 6 · 17 pages

We prove conjectured independent-set partition-function and coefficient bounds in intervals near both ends of the parameter range for regular uniform hypergraphs, with stability results. For degree two, the partition-function bound holds at every nonnegative activity.
