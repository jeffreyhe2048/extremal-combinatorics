# Weighted Independent Sets in Latin Square Hypergraphs

**Jialin He** · Manuscript 10 · 10 pages

We obtain a uniform two-term weighted independent-set expansion for Latin square hypergraphs and determine its sharp activity interval. We also identify the optimal exponential rate of the remaining contribution and the growth rate attainable by Latin lifts of a fixed base hypergraph.
