# Independent Sets in 3-Uniform 3-Regular Hypergraphs

**Jialin He** · Manuscript 7 · 16 pages

We prove that every simple three-uniform three-regular hypergraph on $n$ vertices has at most $253^{n/9}$ independent sets, with equality exactly for disjoint unions of the conjectured nine-vertex construction. The paper also proves a partition-function comparison and component stability throughout the activity interval $[2/3,5/3]$.
